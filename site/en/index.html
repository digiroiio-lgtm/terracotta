Terracotta Tiles: The Art of Earth

Welcome to the world of TERRACOTTA, where the tradition of natural clay meets craftsmanship to create terracotta tiles of character. Explore six categories, twelve product types and twelve earth colours for indoor and outdoor spaces.

The Art of Earth

TERRACOTTA turns earth, water and fire into surfaces with character. Our collection is organised into six categories and twelve product types, brought together by a palette of twelve earthy colours, from Sand and Clay to Olive, Ocean and Ivory.

Decorative Terracotta Tiles

Decorative Terracotta Tiles bring pattern, rhythm and texture to floors and walls. Hexagon Terracotta Tiles create geometric compositions that work as a full surface or as a feature area, while Decorative Relief Tiles add embossed, textured and patterned surfaces to every project. Colours and formats for each design will be confirmed with verified product data, so decorative areas can be planned to contrast with or complement the surrounding floors and walls.

Terracotta Wall Tiles: Character for Every Wall

TERRACOTTA Wall Tiles bring the warmth of natural clay to interior and exterior walls. Terracotta Wall Cladding creates surfaces with depth and texture, while Custom Artisan Tiles are developed in project-specific sizes, shapes and patterns.
Explore the twelve shades of the TERRACOTTA Colour Collection, from Sand and Rose to Graphite and Ivory, and discover the possibilities for your walls.

Steps & Copings: Details that Complete the Space

TERRACOTTA Steps & Copings complete floors with the same natural material. Terracotta Stair Treads cover steps and stairs, while Terracotta Skirting creates a clean transition between floor and wall.
Coordinated elements help stairs, floors and walls stay in harmony, from the first step to the last, in a single natural material.

Outdoor Terracotta Tiles: Natural Clay for Outdoor Spaces

TERRACOTTA offers Outdoor Terracotta Tiles for gardens, verandas, patios and terraces. Terracotta Brick Tiles bring long, narrow formats to outdoor floors, and Terracotta Pavers are designed for paving outdoor areas.
Explore earthy colours and natural formats to create a warm and functional outdoor space.
