Grace Atelier

Grace Atelier is a design tool where material, texture, and graphics merge into three distinct essences—Grace, Grace Fossil, and Grace Lithos—interacting within the same color palette to open up new horizons for interior design.

Fragments of Grace

The culmination of the path traced by Grace Atelier is represented by Fragments of Grace, the mosaic system designed by Margherita Rui for Terracotta. Four mosaics balancing Modernism and Art Deco influences interact with the Grace Atelier collection, opening up new compositional possibilities. Four distinct designs where modular geometries, woven patterns, metallic effects, and glazed surfaces transform wall coverings into an architectural element.

ULTRAmatt and ULTRAsilky

Two exclusive technologies, one innovative vision of surfaces.

ULTRAmatt is a new anti-glare surface finish, the result of Terracotta’s ongoing research, developed within a master’s thesis project in collaboration with the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia.

ULTRAsilky is an innovative technology, developed and perfected in Terracotta’s laboratories, which expands the applications of satin-finished porcelain stoneware while enhancing its surface technical performance.

Marble Effect: Timeless Luxury and Refinement

Terracotta's Porcelain Marble Effect Tiles collection celebrates the timeless beauty of marble, faithfully reproducing its veins and shades. Ideal for creating elegant and sophisticated environments, without sacrificing the practicality and resistance of porcelain stoneware.
Available in various finishes and sizes, our marble-effect line adapts perfectly to any furnishing style, from classic to contemporary.

Wood Effect: Warmth and Naturalness for Your Spaces

Terracotta's Wood-Effect Porcelain Tiles bring the warmth and authenticity of wood into your spaces. Perfect for creating welcoming and relaxing atmospheres, these tiles combine the natural aesthetics of wood with the resistance and practicality of porcelain stoneware.
Choose from various wood types and shades to find the solution that best suits your taste and project.

Outdoor Porcelain Tiles: Beauty and Durability for Your Outdoor Spaces

Terracotta offers a wide range of Outdoor porcelain tiles. Resistant to weathering, anti-slip, and easy to clean, our tiles are the ideal solution for paving terraces, balconies, and gardens.
Choose from various finishes and sizes to create an elegant and functional outdoor space.
